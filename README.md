# 🏗️ Data Center Resilience & Technology Risk

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focused on the resilience of critical business services and the technology supporting them across data centers, infrastructure platforms and external providers.

I worked across infrastructure, cybersecurity, application, business continuity and provider teams. My role combined technical project management with active involvement in architecture, recovery, cybersecurity and technology risk discussions.

---

## 🎯 Business Challenge

Critical business services depended on complex technology chains across applications, networks, infrastructure, data centers and third-party providers.

The main challenge was to understand these dependencies, identify weak points and determine whether existing redundancy, failover and recovery concepts were sufficient for critical services.

Cybersecurity incidents and DORA requirements were also considered as part of the overall resilience assessment.

---

## 🧩 My Role

I managed the project across several technical workstreams and worked closely with subject matter experts from infrastructure, cybersecurity, applications and business continuity.

I was not the engineer implementing the technical solutions myself, but I was actively involved in the technical discussions, challenged assumptions, aligned different expert views and helped translate them into decisions and improvement measures.

---

## 🔧 Key Responsibilities

- Managed The Project Across Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Applications, Business Continuity And External Providers

- Analyzed Critical Services And Technology Dependencies Together With Technical SMEs

- Discussed Data Center Architecture, Redundancy, Failover, RTO And RPO With Infrastructure And Application Teams

- Assessed Cybersecurity, Technology And Third-Party Risks Across Critical Service Dependencies

- Worked With SMEs To Define And Prioritize Practical Improvements For Resilience, Recovery And Security

---

## 🏗️ Solution Approach

We looked at the service end-to-end rather than reviewing individual technology components in isolation.

```text
Critical Business Service
          │
          ▼
      Applications
          │
          ▼
 Shared Technology Services
          │
     ┌────┼────┐
     ▼    ▼    ▼
 Network IAM  Data
     │
     ▼
Infrastructure Platforms
     │
     ▼
Data Centers / Providers
```

This helped us identify dependencies that were not always visible from the perspective of a single technical team.

Together with the relevant SMEs, we reviewed failure scenarios such as:

- Data Center Or Infrastructure Outages
- Network Or Shared Service Failures
- Cybersecurity Incidents
- Third-Party Provider Disruptions
- Failed Or Delayed Recovery

We then compared the technical recovery concepts with business requirements such as service criticality, RTO and RPO.

---

## 🛡️ Cybersecurity & Technology Risk

Cybersecurity was treated as part of operational resilience rather than as a separate topic.

The discussions included security architecture, IAM, network segmentation, privileged access, cyber recovery and dependencies on shared or external technology services.

Technology risks were assessed based on their potential business impact, current mitigation and remaining exposure.

---

## 🏛️ DORA & Regulatory Context

The project was performed in a regulated financial services environment.

DORA requirements were considered particularly in relation to:

- ICT Business Continuity And Disaster Recovery
- Critical ICT Services
- Resilience Testing
- ICT Third-Party Providers
- Concentration And Dependency Risks

I worked with the technical teams to translate these requirements into practical resilience and recovery considerations.

Relevant standards also included ISO 27001, NIST and internal cybersecurity requirements.

- Identified Single Points Of Failure And Important Concentration Risks

- Strengthened Alignment Between Business Recovery Requirements And Technical Recovery Capabilities

- Improved Cybersecurity And Third-Party Risk Considerations Within Resilience Planning

- Created Clearer Priorities For Technical Remediation And Follow-Up Actions
